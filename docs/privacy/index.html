BibleLingo Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

BibleLingo is an app for learning French, Spanish and English by reading the New Testament. It is made by Sephora Jean ("we"). This policy explains what information the BibleLingo app for Android and the BibleLingo website collect, why, who else handles it, and how to delete it.

In short: you can use BibleLingo without an account, and then your progress stays on your device. If you sign in with Google, we keep your name, email address and learning progress so you can continue on other devices. We don't show ads, don't sell your information, and don't use analytics or tracking tools. You can delete your account at any time in the app.

Information we collect

If you use BibleLingo without signing in

Your progress (the chapters and steps you've finished, the days you practiced, your chosen language and listening speed) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us.

If you sign in with Google

When you choose Sign in with Google, Google shares these details with us, and we store them:

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your Google account ID, a number that identifies your account to us.

We also save your learning progress in your account: the chapters and steps you've finished, the days you practiced, your chosen language, listening speed and last chapter. This lets you continue on another phone or on the website. We never receive your Google password.

When you translate a phrase

When you select words in a chapter and tap Translate, the phrase is sent to our server and then to Google Cloud Translation, which returns the translation. The phrase is not linked to your account. To prevent abuse, our server keeps your device's IP address in memory for up to about ten minutes to limit how many translations one visitor can request. It does not save IP addresses or phrases in a database.

Reading and listening

Bible text and chapter recordings are delivered from our server and our storage provider. Like any website or online service, these providers receive standard technical information with each request, such as your IP address and device type, and may keep it in short-term server logs.

Words read aloud in lessons use your phone's or browser's built-in voice, on your device.

Speaking exercises

The Android app does not record or listen to your voice. On the website, if your browser supports speech recognition, a speaking exercise asks for microphone permission and uses the browser's own speech recognition service, which is provided by your browser's maker (for example, Google for Chrome) under its own privacy policy. We don't receive or store the audio.

How we use your information

  • To sign you in and show your name and email in the app.
  • To save your progress and keep it the same on all your devices.
  • To translate phrases you select.
  • To keep the service working and protect it from abuse.

We don't use your information for advertising, we don't sell or rent it, and we don't share it except with the service providers below, who process it for us to run BibleLingo.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and saved progress until you delete your account. Translation rate-limit records are kept in memory for about ten minutes. Server logs kept by our providers are deleted on their own schedules.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: open Profile, tap Delete account, then tap it again to confirm. This permanently deletes your account, your name, email address and Google account ID, and all progress saved in your account. It also clears that progress from the device you used.

By email: if you no longer have the app, email sephorejean@gmail.com with the subject "BibleLingo account deletion" from the email address you signed in with. We'll delete your account and its data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Progress saved on a device without an account is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Security

All communication between the app and our server uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to our database is restricted to the developer.

Children

BibleLingo is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has signed in, contact us and we'll delete the account.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we'll update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
