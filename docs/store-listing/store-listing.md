## Full description (4000 characters max)

Learn a language by reading the New Testament.

BibleLingo turns every chapter of the New Testament into short lessons. Read real verses, hear them read aloud, learn the words that matter, and practice them, a few minutes a day.

WALK THE PILGRIM'S ROAD
Your path runs through all 27 books, from Matthew to Revelation. Each chapter is a stop along the road, and the landscape changes with the story: the Sea of Galilee, the mountains, the desert roads and Jerusalem. A friendly guide waits at the chapter you're on.

THREE STEPS IN EVERY CHAPTER
• Key words: learn the most useful words in the chapter before you read it.
• Read and listen: read the whole chapter in the language you're learning and hear it read aloud. Tap a word, then another, to select a phrase and see its translation.
• Grammar lesson: practice what the chapter teaches with translation, fill-in-the-blank, word order and speaking exercises.

REAL RECORDINGS
Listen to recorded readings of the chapters in French and Spanish, or to your phone's own voice. Slow it down to 0.5× while you're learning or speed it up to 1.5× as you improve.

THREE LANGUAGES
• Learn French with the Louis Segond 1910 translation.
• Learn Spanish with the Reina-Valera 1909 translation.
• Learn English with the World English Bible, from French or Spanish.

BUILD A DAILY HABIT
Finish a step each day to grow your streak. Hearts are unlimited, so you can keep going for as long as you like.

YOUR PROGRESS, EVERYWHERE
Use it as a guest with no account, or sign in with Google to keep your progress in your account and pick up where you left off on another phone or on the web.

No ads.

French recordings used by permission of Trésor sonore (tresorsonore.com), through Bible Brain. Bible texts are in the public domain.
